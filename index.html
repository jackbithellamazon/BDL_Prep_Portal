Tidy up — Sarah’s notes in the packing column

Until 8 Sep, a note Sarah typed when she sent a row to Jack was written into Packing notes — the warehouse’s own column. That is where the order references and “refund issued in gift card” came from. New ones go to the VA note. This moves the old ones across: the text is copied to the VA note with who wrote it and when, then removed from packing notes. Nothing is deleted.

—checking…

Tidy up — closed rows that never balanced

A close-off never used to touch the row’s units or status. So a row Sarah closed as wrong SKU or arrived and correct kept showing as In-Transit with units still out — on the Prep Sheet and in the In Transit total, but on nobody’s list. From this build a close settles the row as it closes. This applies the same rule to the ones already stuck: units Sarah traced to another SKU are accounted for and the row comes off the Prep Sheet; units nothing accounts for go to Becki to check the shelves. It also clears “Late” labels left on rows that owe nothing. Nothing is deleted. Rows parked on a date, rows already with Becki, and refunds Jack is chasing are not touched.
